## Fringe

The autumn season already pleads for heavier fabrics, as we see more suede, leather and textured layers, so a new fringe era was only natural. There is a heavy emphasis on texture this season, because of the post-summer heat wave where we had to strip it back, people are looking to uplift their layers to be more of a powerful statement. Don’t mistake the fringe trend for a simple detail, instead think of the Khaite Lotus fringe bag, that trails from shoulder to feet. Whether an art-deco 20s fringe ALAÏA skirt or a 70s western Chloé cape, AW26 showed us exactly what we are in for.

Younger me would have been thrilled to know my silver, Hannah Montana fringe bag that I clung onto every time I left the house is as fashionable as ever, and 20 year old me is excited to bring that back (maybe not that exact bag).

## Funnel neck Jacket

A high collar, a turtleneck, a striped scarf - every autumn we find a new way to keep warm, this year it is the funnel neck jacket. On the Copenhagen AW26 Forza Collective runway we had the pleasure of seeing Kristoffer Kongshaug’s eye-high, popping red collar on a cool grey, asymmetric jacket. From Balenciaga, we have had the burgundy, high collar cape and from Chloé we had the khaki, funnel neck reiteration of the mac in classic Chloé, victorian silhouette.


## Kitten heel

As the seasons change, usually so do shoe lengths. However, it seems this autumn, instead of striving for high boots, we are keeping our shoes short. From kitten heels, clogs, and loafers, the only change is the socks that will start peeping from underneath as the weather cools. Whether it’s Onitsuka tigers having a kitten heel transformation or Conner Ives AW26 tassel loafer. After a summer of havaianas, no one is ready to strap into a pair of boots, so this might just be fashion’s little gift to us all.

## Olive Green Apocalypse

Grey, Ecru, Black and White are not the only neutrals, and now more than ever we know that Olive Green is the new neutral. In particular, AW26 showed us the burnt colour overtake of styling, with olive green taking center stage. Pairing with bright red, butter yellow, baby blue, dusty pink and everyone’s favourite turquoise, olive green will be key in keeping the colorful outfits coming, but toning it down as the lighting dims. From the fabulous, floor length coat on show for Valentino, a Saint Laurent lacy olive dress to Chanel, crocodile texture bag - everyone wants a taste of olive.

## Baby Doll Dress

Everyone assumes autumn is about covering up and saying goodbye to the short dresses of summer. But AW26 says it doesn’t have to be this way. Chloé showed off several flowy, gothesque baby doll dresses, showing how to change a summer essential into an autumn staple. Even Miu Miu showcased new iterations of the babydoll dress, from a tan, art-deco encrusted example to a simple LBD, with a bow at the front. And don’t worry, when the cold comes, the vibrant, pattern tights come out - making us ever more excited to have a little summer dress on hand.
