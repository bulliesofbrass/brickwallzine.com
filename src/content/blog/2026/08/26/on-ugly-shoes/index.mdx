The consensus seems to be that the following items just don't make the cut:

## 1. Clogs/mules

<Image src={_001} style="height: 600px; width: auto; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />

Birkenstock did a good job of giving the clog the ultimate glow up when the Bostons took the fashion world by storm - how many years ago now? But I believe these Next studded ones are their chic younger sister. Picture them with a fun pair of neon socks in summer with a slip dress. They could so be worn to the office too, frappé in hand. I'm all for black leather in and Bostons in the bin.

## 2. Loretta leather slip ons

<Image src={_002} style="height: 600px; width: auto; display: block; margin: 0 auto;" />

Grandma's go-to? But who said that isn't in? Maybe ditch the florals when planning an outfit with these, to avoid the granny allegations, but I seriously believe in the styling power of these shoes. I'd pair them with something baggy; black or white bloomer trousers with a lace finish, fitted red tee and a hair accessory (bow or bandana).

## 3. Clarks

<SideBySide>
	<Image src={_003} />
	<Image src={_004} />
</SideBySide>

Clarks either fall into the senior fashion category or back to school - it takes me back to the days of the foot measuring device (google search 'junior foot gauge'). It sure did a good Daisy Doll school shoe and Lelli Kelly back in the day, but has it still got it? Absolutely. My most recent pair of ballet pumps are Clarks Originals and I'm obsessed with the bow and topstitching details.

## 4. Wallabees

<Image src={_005} style="aspect-ratio: 1/1; object-fit: cover; object-position: top;" />

Don't disrespect the Wally's! I still don't have a pair and I'm not happy about it. They will be on my Christmas list '26. They're the OGs, it's written all over, and I love their androgynous quality. This photo was taken in December so they have sat in my camera roll for nearly a year and are waiting to be bought. The colourways are endless (I recently spotted a leopard print/black version - the most Scarlet shoe I think I have ever laid eyes on).

## 5. Walking shoes

<Image src={_006} />

The biggest offence ever was when my nan would take me somewhere like Mountain Warehouse (no disrespect) and insist that I needed a pair of 'proper' walking shoes for holiday. This was peak 2016 era when I was entering 'teen' and walking/Jesus sandals couldn't be less in if they tried (according to me anyway). Well, times have changed and I have recently adopted a pair of my mum's. Hear me out, NEVER with socks and strictly for festivals/hot countries. I think reaching maturity is changing your opinion on these summer shoes that society has conditioned us into hating.
