On my evening walk again.

To clear my head.

I wonder if the woman in front knows

her heels could pass for Pucci 78.

But she walked straight past the little vintage shop.

Without a second look.

All these antiques,

waiting for a second chance.

Behind the ‘FOR RENT’ sign there’s an art deco lamp calling my name.

If objects get a second chance - do people?

I think about the last “sorry I didn’t see this message text”

And the 3 exact ones I sent before.

Thank god I have good friends.

Maybe if second chances weren’t real that message wouldn’t be the only one I know how to send.

I just got an email,

another link I forgot to unsubscribe from.

Not the reply from the job I’ve been replaying in my head.

The pinstripe suit, block heels, walking straight to my desk,

typing away.

The image of it replays in my head as the rejection email is still yet to come.

I’ve just tripped on the same bakery corner step.

Someone ought to fix that.

Funny how we are equipped for different jobs.

I could picture myself at the morning shift at the bakery,

but not for one second did I think I could pave this path.

Oh god I hate this song.

I click skip. And two more times.

I love all the songs on my playlist but they start to wear out.

I think about the pin holding my worn out dress together.

And how if my γιαγιά1 were here she would pull out the old Dutch biscuit tin

and thread her needle.

I could too.

But my needles aren’t as sharp.

If only I could take it in with a thread of words.

That’s all that seems to hold most things together.

Our recipes, our records,

our love, our resentment.

If your language was lost tomorrow,

what would hold it together for you?

I’ve reached the sea now.

Time to walk back,

since the benches are taken.

I’m not sure I cleared my head.

But I’ll try tomorrow instead.

1 Greek word for grandma.
