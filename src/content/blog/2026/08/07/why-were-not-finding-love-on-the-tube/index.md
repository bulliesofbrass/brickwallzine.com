As I’m sat in Gail’s with my wired headphones plugged in, I realise that I am indeed a victim, yet I proceed to write the article anyway.

Firstly, allow me to make it clear that everything is okay avec modération - that’s in small doses. Wires don’t have to be a public enemy. My optimal headphone experience is hitting shuffle on a playlist I’ve spent hours curating, I’m still not in my podcast era yet admittedly. Yes I love music, who doesn’t? And most the time I like to handpick my EPs for the tube journey, I hop on the Piccadilly line with my tunes all queued up.

But

Sometimes I do think about my favourite films and the logistics of fate…and the fact that I’m single. How will I ever have my Sliding Doors or Serendipity moment if I’m constantly plugged in? Did the guy I briefly made eye contact with at the platform actually intend to tell me my shoe needed lacing up to then go on and say ‘by the way I’m [blank] and I think you’re really cool’.

‘Oh you’re local! Do you have a favourite place to eat around here?’

‘How about Tuesday at 7pm?’

I mean does that actually happen? I’m yet to find out. But if it does work out the way it did for Gwyneth Paltrow and Kate Beckinsale, I’m afraid I wouldn’t know most the time. For all I know I could have had four lovers confess their love for me, all while I was too busy thinking about the lyrics to Inbetween Days by The Cure.

It’s not even just about love - don’t think you taken ones can get away scot free! A smile goes a long way but a small conversation goes way further! Learning about people’s lives is beautiful and enriching, especially if you’re a writer and you need some external facets of information to build a character. You could cast anyway you meet on the tube in your upcoming debut novel, but you won’t miss out on much if you save the listening for when you’re home alone cooking up a storm.

So I’m not saying ditch the wires, they can feature in the ‘what’s in my bag’ on a day trip to the city, but consider. Think about the person you are becoming when they’re connected to your device and filter out the background noise of the train. You might as well paint ‘Don’t talk to me’ across your face. You’re not only missing out perhaps on the outfit complement you never knew you needed, but also a potential date! Romance is not dead and we can start living like we’re in the movies - that’s if you want to.
