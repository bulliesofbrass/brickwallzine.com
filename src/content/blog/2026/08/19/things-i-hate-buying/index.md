Now I promise I'm not stingy but for some reason I gain no pleasure whatsoever from purchasing the following items (slight exception for #2). Essential? Yes, but do I get any satisfaction from tapping my card and then seeing that dreaded Santander notification? Not really.

## 1. Mascara

I rely heavily on my dried up collection of Maybelline mascara, and that Dior Overcurl which I refuse to chuck away as the tube is so pretty - and they're all past their sell-by date. For me nothing beats M.A.C Gigablack (the mini blue one) and I'm deluding myself into thinking I'm getting some of that black substance out - every day. There's just not enough of it to warrant £22 and I've truly convinced myself that mascara never runs out. I must stop putting off that all-important Boots trip I guess.

## 2. Books (with a high RRP)

Hm. Don't get me wrong there is no better feeling than finishing a book, posting it on Goodreads and then buying the next one in a Waterstones or Foyles. I did spend £9.99 on a García Márquez book recently with only 101 pages - basically a short story with nowhere near enough bang for buck. I guess this one is personal to me as I admittedly have a track record of not finishing what I start, a habit of which I'm trying to get out. I think the only place where buying a brand new paperback is totally, 100% acceptable is at the airport. I'd prefer to strike gold in a charity shop, like a big Oxfam; a 10 pound note spent there guarantees a whole bundle of exciting new reads.

## 3. Wrigley's Gum TUB

There's nothing more disappointing than getting to the end of a tub of Wrigley's Cool Breeze gum. When I'm down to just 3, I consider the corner shop trip but end up putting it off until I'm carrying an empty container. I've had to put emphasis on the 46 piece TUB rather than the strips you can buy (they're a lot easier to spend on but often get lost before I can finish the pack). Internet puts tub RRP at £2.50, but I vividly remember walking into a Co-op recently and being charged almost £4 - that's meal deal money surely.

## 4. Perfume

Okay I am stingy when it comes to spending on perfume. I allow myself 2 sprays max for a day to day sitch and 3 for an occasion. I like to savour my bottle, why? Because I know it won't be getting replaced once empty. I haven't ever experienced the luxury of walking into a department store and sampling the fragrances with the intention of actually buying one. Usually it's to get an idea for my Christmas list. Maybe I haven't reached peak womanhood yet? Or more truthfully, I just don't think I have the funds for 100ml of La Vie est Belle in my early 20s.

## 5. A bottle of water

Rarely will I enter a Tesco for a bottle of water when out in the city. This is because I will always prioritise bag space for my reusable water bottle and fill it up to the top. I'd honestly rather queue for tap water in a Caffè Nero if I'm running low - a method that saves on pennies and pollution!
